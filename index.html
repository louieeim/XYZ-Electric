Help Center
Billing
How is my electric bill computed?
Your bill is computed by multiplying consumption (kWh) by the applicable rate. Charges include energy, distribution, transmission, metering, universal charge, plus 12% VAT, minus any applicable subsidies like the Lifeline subsidy.
View your bill breakdown →
When is my bill's due date?
Bills are due 20 days from the statement date. Your due date is shown on the Current Bill screen and in your inbox. Set up auto-pay to never miss a due date.
Set up auto-pay →
What happens if I miss the due date?
A 2% surcharge applies to unpaid balances after the due date. Continued non-payment may result in service disconnection after formal notice, per ERC regulations. Reconnection fees apply after disconnection.
Why is my bill higher than usual?
Common reasons: increased usage (appliances, longer hours), estimated billing (meter not read), rate adjustments approved by ERC, or a billing correction from a previous period. Check the Usage screen to compare months.
Compare monthly usage →
Payments
Where can I pay my bill?
Pay via GCash, Maya, BDO/BPI/Metrobank online banking, Bayad Center, SM Bills Payment, 7-Eleven, and Robinsons Business Centers. In-app payment takes less than 30 seconds.
Pay now →
How do I set up auto-pay?
Go to Bills → tap the Auto-pay row → choose your preferred payment method (GCash, Maya, or BDO) and toggle it on. Your bill will be paid automatically on the due date each month.
Set up auto-pay →
I paid but my account still shows unpaid. Why?
GCash and Maya payments reflect instantly. Bank transfers may take 1–2 business days. Over-the-counter payments (7-Eleven, Bayad) may take up to 3 business days. Keep your receipt/reference number.
Dispute a payment →
Outages
How do I report a power outage?
Tap Services → Report power outage, or call our 24/7 hotline at 165-02. Include your account number for faster dispatch.
Report outage →
How long does it take to restore power?
Restoration targets: routine faults within 4 hours, major outages within 24–48 hours, typhoon-related within 72 hours. Actual times vary by damage extent. Check the Outage Map for live updates.
View outage map →
What is a planned outage?
Planned outages are scheduled for maintenance work. We notify affected customers at least 48 hours in advance via SMS, inbox notification, and the Outage Map screen.
Account
How do I register my account in the app?
Tap "Register" on the login screen, enter your account number (printed on your bill), fill in your personal details, and verify via OTP sent to your mobile number. You can also sign up with Google.
Register now →
How do I update my contact details?
Go to Account → Personal information to update your email, mobile number, or address. Changes take effect within 24 hours and you'll receive a confirmation via SMS.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your registered mobile number. You'll receive an OTP to reset your password. The link expires in 10 minutes.
Metering
How do I read my electricity meter?
Read the five dials from left to right, noting each pointer's digit. The difference between two consecutive readings gives your consumption in kWh for that billing period.
My meter reading seems wrong. What should I do?
Submit an actual reading request through the app. Our team will verify and adjust your bill if the official reading differs. You can also take a photo of your meter as evidence.
Submit meter reading →
